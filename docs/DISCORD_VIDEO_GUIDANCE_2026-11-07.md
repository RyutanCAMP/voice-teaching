# Discord organizer guidance for demo video (2026-11-07 shared notes)

Source: six screenshots shared by project owner on Nov 7, showing organizer replies dated Nov 4-6. This is a working summary, not a replacement for official rules.

## Confirmed organizer responses
1. **Claude character / Anthropic branding:** Avoid incorporating Anthropic brand elements into the video without prior approval. Organizer recommends appearing personally and explaining the project instead.
2. **Narration:** A video with subtitles and BGM but no spoken explanation is permitted by the rules, although delivery and demonstration affect evaluation.
3. **Discord survey:** Optional non-promotional surveys may be shared in #交流-socials; avoid spam, solicitation, and PR.
4. **AI-generated art / external artist work:** Organizer says design-only imagery can be used, but the judged deliverable is work created with Claude. Clearly identify origins and tools in README. This does not mean external images are automatically eligible as judged work.
5. **Synthetic speech:** Partial Japanese synthesized narration is allowed.

## Actions for Voice Teaching
- Keep the recorded real-robot demonstration central; show voice instruction, safety check/approval, and resulting motion without implying autonomous unsafe operation.
- Prefer owner's own narration where practical; captions/BGM are optional.
- Check title cards, credits and imagery for unauthorized Anthropic branding or character appearances.
- In README, distinguish Claude Code implementation from externally generated design assets, photography and other tools; credit actual tools only after verification.
- Do not change app code or claim final compliance based solely on this summary.
- Review against latest submission rules before publishing.
